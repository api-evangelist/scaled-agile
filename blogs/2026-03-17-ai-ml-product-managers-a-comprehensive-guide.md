However, the position is almost unrecognizable from its early days and is still changing rapidly. Much of the reason for this is evolving technologies. Currently, AI and machine learning are transforming the way we conduct business, and our traditional roles must evolve with them to accommodate this new technology.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">In product development, especially, AI/ML is moving from research endeavours into practical application, and organizations require support from specialists in this area to successfully envision, create, and deploy AI products.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">This is where the role of an <strong>AI ML Product Manager</strong> is becoming increasingly crucial.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-what-is-an-ai-product-manager">What Is an AI Product Manager?</h2>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">A traditional PM oversees the vision and strategy for a product and defines a roadmap for developing it. An <a href="https://framework.scaledagile.com/blog/new-safe-skills-available-integrating-ai-into-product-owner-and-scrum-master-roles">AI product manager</a> does the same thing, but specifically for products where artificial intelligence or ML is a core part of the product itself, not merely a tool used by the team during development. This change, however, is enough to alter the role significantly.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><a href="https://scaledagile.com/blog/ai-in-product-development/">Implementing AI within the product makes the development process</a> more dynamic, as the products evolve with the data they process. Essentially, they have the capacity to learn. As such, their development process must be continually adaptive, which requires a new skillset and mindset.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">That’s why the role of an AI product manager is distinct; they are responsible not just for what the application does today, but for how it learns and changes over time. This means balancing standard product strategy with an <a href="/blog/data-driven-product-strategy/">understanding of data</a> and machine learning behavior, as well as ongoing model iteration.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">A few unique core aspects of the role of an AI PM include:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Data-driven decision-making: AI products rely on data, so PMs must be well-versed in how this is collected and processed, and understand model training</li>



<li>Model lifecycle management: AI and ML models are anything but static. They require PMs to guide continual iteration and retraining to keep it aligned with its objectives</li>



<li>Cross-functional collaboration: AI teams will include personnel from many disciplines, from engineers to analysts. The PM bridges these roles and ensures everyone collaborates smoothly towards shared goals</li>
</ul>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">The role of an AI product manager is not only related to technical AI use in the products you’re developing; it’s more about connecting AI/ML potential with business value, <a href="https://scaledagile.com/blog/what-is-ai-native/">adopting an AI‑Native mindset</a> that treats AI as a fundamental part of organizational decision-making and workflow design. They must assess entire value streams, identifying where <a href="https://framework.scaledagile.com/product-development-flow-discipline/harnessing-customer-feedback-competency">AI products can improve customer experiences</a> or enhance processes, rather than focusing just on machine learning components or features.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-what-s-the-difference-between-traditional-vs-ai-product-management">What’s The Difference Between Traditional vs AI Product Management?</h2>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">As mentioned earlier, AI/ML product management has unique requirements due to the dynamic nature of AI-driven products. This isn’t the only way the role contrasts with traditional PM responsibilities, however. Here are several significant ways in which they differ:</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-communication-and-culture">Communication and Culture</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Although product management has always involved translating features into business outcomes, it becomes even more important when AI is involved. An AI-Native culture is one where responsible AI practices and ongoing learning are commonplace and integrated into decision-making.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">To develop such a culture, it is necessary to bridge technical complexity with a holistic business understanding that doesn’t just include value creation but also ethical considerations, regulatory compliance, and the responsible use of AI in ways that protect users and society.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-unpredictable-outcomes-and-development-lifecycle">Unpredictable Outcomes and Development Lifecycle</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">In traditional product management, products and their features generally behave as expected after they’re released, so it’s easy to predict timelines. This is not always the case with AI-driven solutions. Their results can vary based on factors such as training data and real-world interactions. As a result, PMs must be prepared to adapt and iterate to keep outcomes aligned with expectations, which can affect timelines. This is where our <a href="https://scaledagile.com/certification/product-owner-product-manager/">AI-Empowered PO/PM certification</a> can help you.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-success-metrics">Success Metrics</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Traditionally, the success of a product was measured by standard metrics like adoption and engagement rates, as well as revenue. While these are naturally still relevant to AI products, there are more organization-centric benchmarks to judge success on, too. These include the fairness and trustworthiness of AI outputs, as well as their explainability and business impact.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">For example, an AI‑driven recommendation engine might be evaluated not only on click-through or conversion rates but also on whether its suggestions are equitable across different user groups and whether product teams can understand and explain why specific recommendations are made. These metrics ensure both business value and responsible AI use.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-organizational-impact">Organizational Impact</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>AI/ML product managers</strong> don’t just influence individual projects or features; instead, they shape entire workflows and value streams. They help businesses embed AI into operations at scale and automate decisions. Essentially, they guide organizations to think with AI, not just use it as a tool. This company-wide influence is necessary because AI’s value is realized only when it transforms business processes, not just individual products.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-ethics-and-regulations">Ethics and Regulations</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Beyond compliance, traditional PMs don’t have much focus on governance, and accountability largely centres on product success and adoption. An AI product manager, on the other hand, must consider everything from bias and fairness to transparency, privacy, and security (everything that makes up responsible and ethical AI practices). Additionally, they’re accountable for model errors and societal impacts of AI outputs. Put simply, the stakes are higher.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-ai-ml-product-manager-role-and-responsibilities">AI <strong>ML Product Manager</strong> Role and Responsibilities</h2>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">The specific responsibilities of an AI Product Manager will vary greatly depending on their industry and use cases. However, there are some obligations that unite all roles in this area. Generally, these all come under the umbrella of balancing tech with business, but here are some specific commitments:</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-identifying-where-ai-holds-real-value">Identifying Where AI Holds Real Value</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">An AI PM’s role is not to apply AI wherever possible; it’s far more considered than this. Instead, they must evaluate whether a problem actually needs AI to be solved and whether the use of intelligence would meaningfully outperform simpler alternatives. This process sets an AI-Native organization apart from others, ensuring that AI initiatives align business processes, data, and decision-making so that they drive consistent, measurable outcomes.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-driving-enterprise-scale-adoption">Driving Enterprise-Scale Adoption</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Many AI initiatives fail because they don’t integrate: they remain siloed. A product manager holds the task of ensuring these products move beyond proof-of-concept by planning integration across functions and aligning workflows to prevent barriers to scaling.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-aligning-people-with-technology">Aligning People With Technology</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">AI solutions deliver real value only when teams know how to use them effectively. AI/ML product managers are responsible for embedding AI into workflows and decision-making, ensuring it supports business goals. They coordinate cross-functional teams to help organizations adopt a mindset where AI is a tool for meaningful outcomes, not just experimentation.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-managing-ethics-and-risk">Managing Ethics and Risk</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">As we’ve noted, there are significant amounts of ethical and regulatory considerations to prioritize when managing an AI product lifecycle. AI/ML managers are responsible for anticipating potential failures, such as biases, and putting guardrails in place to maintain fairness and transparency. This also involves coordinating with legal and compliance teams.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">By ingraining these practices into development, PMs balance innovation with accountability and ensure products deliver value safely.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-facilitating-continuous-learning-nbsp">Facilitating Continuous Learning&nbsp;</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">One of an AI product manager’s main roles is ensuring that their products improve over time by effectively utilizing AI models. To do this, they must establish feedback loops that connect user interaction, the resulting business outcomes, and the AI model’s performance. Insight from these will guide updates and keep AI accurate and aligned with evolving organizational goals.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-ai-ml-product-management-guiding-principles">AI/ML Product Management Guiding Principles</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-why-gt-what">Why &gt; What</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Naturally, the quality of a completed AI product is important. But <em>why</em> it’s created is of equal or greater concern for product managers in this space. What objective will this solution help you achieve, and will it help organizations become closer to being <a href="/blog/what-is-ai-native/">AI Native</a>?</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-outcomes-gt-metrics">Outcomes &gt; Metrics</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Quantitative metrics are always important in measuring success, but an AI product’s impact is measured through more than just hard figures like uptake and revenue. While these shouldn&#8217;t be ignored, it’s vital to focus on the outcomes that these deliverables create, such as improved decision-making, that drive business value.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-responsibility-gt-speed">Responsibility &gt; Speed</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Usually, fast delivery periods are a differentiating factor that helps businesses stay competitive: the faster you can deliver a product, the greater your chances of staying ahead of the market.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">While this is still important in AI product development, the priority is ensuring ethics and transparency before launch. Responsible AI fosters long-term trust and enhances adoption, and should therefore be your primary concern.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-integration-gt-isolation">Integration &gt; Isolation</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">In traditional product development, features can often succeed on their own (e.g. a new UI element or reporting dashboard). While integration matters, siloed products can still bring value. In contrast, AI delivers the greatest benefits when embedded across every aspect of your business, augmenting human capabilities, and enhancing workflows and decision-making while scaling impact.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-why-demand-for-ai-and-machine-learning-pm-roles-is-growing">Why Demand for AI and Machine Learning PM Roles is Growing</h2>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">AI has developed significantly in a short period of time, and its benefits are evident across industries. AI-driven products improve efficiency and accuracy, and therefore allow businesses to improve their performance. But to achieve these results, product development needs to be managed with care and expertise, hence the rapidly increasing demand for quality AI/ML PMs who can leverage AI effectively.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">With AI adoption among companies now at around <a href="https://www.intuition.com/ai-stats-every-business-must-know-in-2026/">72%</a>, product managers with artificial intelligence expertise are now highly sought after across a range of industries.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">But the initial buzz of implementing AI in any way possible and experimenting with results has now worn off. Today, it’s no longer a niche technology; it’s becoming central to how businesses compete and operate.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">As a result, organizations need professionals who can confidently translate AI’s capabilities into user-friendly products that can be embedded into their workflows and inform decision-making. They’re actively seeking product managers who have the skills to bridge the gap between data science and business value.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-how-to-become-an-ai-product-manager">How to Become an AI Product Manager</h2>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Perhaps you’re already a product manager and <a href="https://scaledagile.com/blog/safe-careers-snapshot/">want to upgrade your role</a>, or you have a distinct interest in AI and feel a desire to focus your working life on making an impact with this technology. Alternatively, if you already hold this position, there are always ways to improve the way you carry out your responsibilities.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Whatever your reasons, there are a few concrete steps you can take to increase your chances of being successful in managing AI/ML products:</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-increase-your-ai-knowledge">Increase Your AI Knowledge</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">You’ll be working alongside many data scientists and ML engineers in this role, so while you don’t need to match their technical expertise, it’s important to have AI literacy.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Learn how models are trained, algorithms are optimized, and how LLMs operate at a high level, for instance. The more you know, the more you can speak the language of your team and make informed product decisions based on how intelligence systems work.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">But building the necessary knowledge extends beyond technical know-how. To truly succeed as a PM, you need to understand how AI can be beneficially ingrained into all day-to-day operations so you can design products that don’t just <em>use</em> intelligence but <em>apply</em> it meaningfully across organizations.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-immerse-yourself-in-ai-courses">Immerse Yourself in AI Courses</h3>
