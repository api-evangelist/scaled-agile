<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-left has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-company-recognizes-peter-pedross-and-odile-moreau-at-annual-safe-amp-ai-summit-in-amsterdam"><em>Company Recognizes Peter Pedross and Odile Moreau at Annual SAFe® &amp; AI Summit in Amsterdam</em></h2>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>BOULDER, Colo., April 15, 2026 — </strong>Scaled Agile, Inc. (SAI), the provider of SAFe®, the world’s most trusted system for business agility, and AI-Native enterprise training solutions, today announced two new additions to its SAFe® Fellow community: Peter Pedross, Founder of PEDCO AG, and Odile Moreau, Strategic Advisor and SAFe Program Consultant Trainer (SPCT) at Scaled Agile, Inc. The Fellows were unveiled at the annual SAFe® &amp; AI Summit in Amsterdam.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">A SAFe® supporter since version 2 in 2012, Pedross created Applied SAFe® and built the only system that maps each version of SAFe® to highly regulated industry standards, including Automotive SPICE, ISO 26262, CMMI, FDA regulations and IEC standards. Organizations such as Porsche, Mercedes, and leading aerospace and medtech companies rely on his work to run their development using SAFe®. Peter has always been committed to understanding SAFe® inside and out and continually pushes for more guidance on risk, regulatory compliance, and quality.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">With over 20 years of experience in IT Service Management, Business Information Management, and Software Engineering across Europe, Moreau has been a high-value contributor since joining SAI, providing guidance on ESG initiatives and Business and Technology Patterns. She has emerged as a champion for applying Agile practices to sustainability outcomes and is a strong advocate for localization and Women in Agile. She delivers consulting and training in four languages: French, English, Dutch, and German.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">&#8220;We’re happy to welcome Peter and Odile to the Fellow community,&#8221; said Andrew Sales, Chief Methodologist and Chief Product Officer at SAI. &#8220;Both bring rare depth, real-world impact, and a genuine passion for advancing SAFe® that will benefit practitioners around the world.&#8221;</p>
