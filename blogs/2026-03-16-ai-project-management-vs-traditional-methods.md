<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0" id="h-what-we-mean-when-we-say-traditional-methods"><strong>What We Mean When We Say Traditional Methods</strong></h2>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Traditional project management doesn&#8217;t necessarily mean anything that happened before the advent of AI. In fact, traditional project management originally meant methods that predate the<a href="https://scaledagile.com/blog/happy-anniversary-agile-manifesto/"> Agile Manifesto</a> and the subsequent development of iterative approaches.&nbsp;</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>Pre-Agile Era</strong></h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Before Agile, there were several project management methods:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>The Waterfall method</strong> is what people usually mean when they&#8217;re referring to traditional project management. Categorized as linear, rigid, and sequential, the Waterfall method is broken down into five stages: Initiation, Planning, Execution, Monitoring, and Closure.</li>



<li>While not its own method, <strong>Gantt charts</strong> are used by PMs to outline the entire scope of a project and help teams better understand the process. Gantt charts are useful in traditional methods because they facilitate project monitoring.</li>



<li><strong>CPPM</strong> or <strong>critical chain project management</strong> (also known as the critical path method) helps PMs keep track of essential resources while they prioritize dependent tasks for maximum efficiency. CCPM is a good strategy to keep an eye on resources so that each task in the critical path has what it needs to reach completion.</li>



<li>The <strong>PERT </strong>(program evaluation and review techniques) method is more focused on timeline analysis. Using this method, the PM calculates the minimum amount of time each individual task needs, and then uses that to determine how long a project will take before dividing up resources.<br /><br /></li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>Pros of Traditional Methods</strong></h3>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Mapping out project plans ahead of time creates clear expectations and makes it easy to <a href="https://scaledagile.com/resources/safe-roi-calculator/" id="https://scaledagile.com/resources/safe-roi-calculator/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank" type="link">estimate costs</a>, workloads, and resources.</li>



<li>Helps everyone on the project clearly understand their responsibilities.</li>



<li>Gives PMs abillity to foresee and therefore mitigate potential risks.</li>



<li>Helps PMs maintain more control of any changes and makes them solely accountable.</li>



<li>Processes and standards are well documented, which helps inform management of other projects.<br /><br /></li>
</ul>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>Cons of Traditional Methods</strong></h4>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Lack of flexibility can lead to increased costs and delays.</li>



<li>Non-collaborative.</li>



<li>Limited customer or user feedback due to the linear nature of the process.<br /><br /></li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>Post-Agile Era</strong></h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">These days, the Agile method is the most popular approach to project management because it is iterative, collaborative, flexible—in other words, <em>agile. </em>There are <a href="https://framework.scaledagile.com/safe-lean-agile-principles" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">12 basic principles of Agile</a>, all of which enforce customer satisfaction, adaptability, and cooperation. Common Agile methods and frameworks include:&nbsp;</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Kanban </strong>is a visual workflow tool designed to help teams limit multitasking by ensuring they only have a certain number of &#8216;in progress&#8217; tasks at a time. Everyone sees the scope of work, which helps teams better manage workflows and see the “big picture”.</li>



<li>The<strong> Scrum </strong>framework is an iterative approach to tackling complex tasks that requires the adoption of certain roles (i.e. <a href="https://scaledagile.com/certification/scrum-master/">Scrum master</a>, developer, and <a href="https://scaledagile.com/certification/product-owner-product-manager/">product owner</a>) and certain Events (e.g., sprint planning, etc.) to deliver usable product increments frequently.</li>



<li>The<strong> Extreme Programming (XP) </strong>method is designed to help teams deliver high-quality software products through <a href="https://scaledagile.com/blog/the-power-of-customer-feedback-in-prioritizing-work/" id="https://scaledagile.com/blog/the-power-of-customer-feedback-in-prioritizing-work/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank" type="link">ongoing customer feedback</a> and short development cycles. This is achieved through tactics like pair programming, frequent communication, and simple design.</li>
</ul>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><br />Whether your management style is based more on traditional or Agile methods, AI project management tools primarily enhance Agile methods but also increasingly support hybrid approaches, as we will see.e Framework for establishing cross-functional teams, clarifying roles, and fostering the relentless improvement that drives business agility.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>What We Mean When We Say AI Project Management</strong></h2>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">You&#8217;d be hard-pressed to find an organization that doesn&#8217;t already have an AI system somehow baked into daily project delivery. When we say AI for project management, we&#8217;re not just talking about automating task assignments, though automation is certainly a part of it. Rather, we&#8217;re talking about a major shift in intelligence, prediction, and optimization.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">AI PM tools use machine learning to enhance business decision-making and efficiency. These tools excel at:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Analyzing historical data for risk prediction (e.g., delays or overruns).</li>



<li>Auto-generating schedules and reports.</li>



<li>Dynamically allocating resources.</li>



<li>Prioritizing tasks.</li>



<li>Integrating with Gantt charts and automatically adjusting task dependencies.<br /></li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>What Could an AI PM Workflow Look Like?</strong></h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Imagine the human project manager types a natural-language prompt into the AI tool. It could be something like, “Help me make a 10-week plan to launch a new feature, with design, build, test, and launch phases for a team of 6.”</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>The AI tool will then&#8230;</strong></p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Generate a timeline and break it down into tasks with their dependencies.</li>



<li>Assign tasks to owners and set milestones.</li>
</ul>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><br />The human PM can then quickly review the project data and tweak any details necessary. As the project tasks are being carried out, all team updates and activities will automatically trigger the AI to auto-update statuses, flag risks like potential delays with confidence scores, and suggest fixes to help teams stay on track and meet their targets. In scaled environments, AI monitors Agile Release Train (ART) work to detect inter-team dependencies and proactively predict bottlenecks.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">In the meantime, the AI tool can draft concise status reports for stakeholders and personalized reminders for individuals to minimize the need for manual communication. If a disruption arises, such as a designer going on sick leave, the project manager can ask the AI tool to help simulate their options.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">When the project reaches its end, the AI tool can compile a full summary report, including details like:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>On-time deliverables.</li>



<li>Delays and slippages, and what caused them.&nbsp;</li>



<li>Resource patterns (e.g., testing is often underestimated by 25%) and risk outcomes.<br /><br /></li>
</ul>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>Benefits of Using AI in Project Management</strong></h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">When combined with human expertise, <a href="/blog/ai-ml-product-managers-guide/">automation and ML</a> can introduce the advantage of speed, accuracy, and productivity, and act as intelligent forecasters to help PMs make better, more cost-effective decisions. In a nutshell:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Optimized planning and scheduling.</li>



<li>Data-backed decision-making.</li>



<li>Enhanced risk assessment and mitigation.</li>



<li>Better resource optimization.</li>



<li>Streamlined task automation.</li>



<li>More accurate cost estimation.</li>



<li>Insightful predictive analytics and accurate forecasting.<br /><br /></li>
</ul>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0"><strong>But Before You Invest in AI…</strong></h2>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">The potential of AI is far-reaching, but without having solid foundations in place, your team may resist adopting it. Becoming <a href="https://scaledagile.com/blog/what-is-ai-native/">AI‑Native means</a> thinking <em>with</em> AI in all aspects of the organization, rather than just seeing it as a tool for course correction.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">But you need to invest in your people first. This means building up their AI literacy and confidence. True adoption happens when project managers and business leaders understand how to frame decisions, interpret data-driven insights, and redesign processes around AI intelligence as a core organizational capability.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">AI works best when combined with human judgment, experience, and contextual awareness. As poor data inputs can lead to unreliable forecasts or unrealistic timelines (for example), using AI systematically and consistently rather than experimentally is a high priority.</p>
