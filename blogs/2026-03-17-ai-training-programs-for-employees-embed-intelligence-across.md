<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Why shouldn’t we rely on free generative AI training resources online?</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Free AI resources can help raise awareness, but they don&#8217;t give you the structure or business context you need to <a href="https://scaledagile.com/certification/achieving-responsible-ai/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">scale AI in a responsible way</a>. </p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">On the other hand, AI-Native courses provide your employees with the shared language and practical frameworks they need to turn experimentation into measurable business results.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Which employees need AI training?</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Many of your employees may already be using AI, but they probably don’t have official skills and expertise in it. As a result, it’s best to comprehensively upskill your whole workforce by offering structured AI training to all employees, from leaders and managers to individual contributors.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Do these programs include compliance training for safe and responsible use of AI?</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Yes. AI-Native courses teach employees more than just how to use AI tools. They also teach them the ethics and compliance requirements they need to consider to use the technology responsibly, making sure they can use the tools safely while still meeting business goals and regulatory standards. By including these practices in effective training, employees use AI strategically, taking ethical considerations into account, and lowering the risk of operational and reputational damage.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">How do AI skills translate into measurable productivity gains across the organization?</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">AI-Native training equips employees with both the knowledge and the confidence to integrate AI into daily workflows and team collaboration. By learning how to apply artificial intelligence strategically (not just leveraging AI technologies), employees can automate routine tasks and uncover insights faster.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">This creates space to focus on higher-value work and boost productivity across teams, including faster project delivery and more consistent outcomes.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">What skills will employees gain to prompt and use AI effectively in real work scenarios?</h3>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">Employees learn how to safely and effectively prompt AI by having a clear understanding of how technologies like AI, machine learning, LLMs, and intelligent agents work. They also learn to use AI in line with business goals and governance expectations, and identify where AI creates real value and redesign everyday workflows.</p>



<p class="has-border-color has-object-object-border-color border-0 has-margin-top-0 has-margin-right-0 has-margin-bottom-0 has-margin-left-0 has-padding-top-0 has-padding-right-0 has-padding-bottom-0 has-padding-left-0">As their skills grow, employees gain the confidence to plan AI solutions from idea to delivery and communicate impact in business-ready terms. This upskilling ensures that AI use leads to measurable, long-lasting results instead of just isolated experimentation.</p>
